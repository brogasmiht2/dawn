# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Apartment residents, urban plant lovers, beginner gardeners, and homeowners with small balconies, windowsills, and compact outdoor spaces seeking to grow fresh herbs, flowers, and greens without cluttering limited floor walkways.

## Product Purpose

BalconyBloomCo provides thoughtful, space-saving gardening essentials—including universal rail-mounted planters, subirrigated self-watering flower boxes, hanging wall systems, and ergonomic tools—that make growing plants easy, clean, and beautiful in small urban spaces.

## Positioning

Grow More in Less Space. Unlike generic gardening suppliers or bulky patio planters, BalconyBloomCo designs specifically for vertical and perimeter urban spaces with damage-free railing mounts, no-drip subirrigation reservoirs, and weatherproof architectural styling.

## Operating Context

- Apartment and condominium balconies with standard metal, wood, or composite railings (1.5" to 4.5").
- High-rise sunlight and wind exposure.
- Strict neighbor rules regarding water dripping onto lower balconies.
- E-commerce shopping experience powered by Shopify Dawn theme on desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Platform: Shopify Dawn 16.0 theme architecture (Liquid, JSON templates, CSS, vanilla JS).
- Full preservation of native Shopify checkout, cart drawer, predictive search, product variants, inventory, and filtering.
- Reusable, merchant-configurable sections and blocks.
- Real Shopify product and collection data; no simulated checkout or mocked products.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

1. **Space-First Utility:** Every item must earn its place in a small footprint by elevating greenery outward or upward.
2. **Urban Cleanliness & Safety:** Zero dripping on neighbors below; secure, non-marking railing attachments.
3. **Beginner Confidence:** Clear guidance, structured product specs, and foolproof care instructions.
4. **Honest Merchant Integrity:** Authentic pricing, genuine availability, and truthful claims without hyperbole.
